## Problem Statement
Unflat needs an owned, low-latency, expressive pt-BR conversational voice. The
immediate commercial question is not "can we build it" (the constitution covers
that) but "what is the narrowest thing someone pays for, and why can't the
incumbent serve it." ElevenLabs is the status quo and is used today via Pedro's
cloned voice, at a cost that breaks the economics of high-volume / experiential
Brazilian work.

## Demand Evidence (behavioral, not interest)
- **Sesc / Mariclea (anchor):** repeat client, 3 years, R$350k+ across 2 projects.
  Last cycle she specced an AI-speech mic for one activation (~R$40k activation,
  6 days/week, ~1-2h speech/day). Unflat **declined the feature purely on
  ElevenLabs cost.** She stayed open to close it next project cycle with Unflat's
  own solution. This is a deferred PO gated on exactly this product.
- **Repeat named pull:** studios 2050 (Gean), Studio Krya; clients/events VTEX Day
  (Monique), Maravalley, Sesc Quitandinha asked for this kind of solution; the
  delivered workaround was Pedro's ElevenLabs-cloned voice (expensive to scale).
- **Quantified status-quo cost:** 1 day (~8h ≈ 480 min) of one S2S agent ≈ the
  entire R$520/mo ElevenLabs Pro quota in a single day; ~R$500/day on a real
  installation. Variable, unpredictable, ~19% of a R$40k activation.
- Weaker signals (logged, not load-bearing): Sec. C&T Rio interest (Tatiana Roque,
  political/champion-dependent); ElevenLabs BR expansion (market validation +
  threat, not demand); FINEP AI-sovereignty call (funding, not demand);
  ElevenLabs startup-program rejection (motivation, zero evidentiary value).

## Status Quo
Clients want an expressive pt-BR signature voice running at activation scale.
Today: clone Pedro's voice on ElevenLabs, eat a variable per-minute cost that is
margin-negative at experiential volume, or decline the feature. Cultural/public
buyers procure **annually with fixed budgets** and reject variable pass-through
cost on principle.

## Target User & Narrowest Wedge
**ICP = the Mariclea archetype:** cultural/experiential institution or studio
with a recurring real budget, annual procurement, buying activations from Unflat,
who wants embedded AI speech but cannot accept variable cost. Not the broke-but-
connected friend (Gean), not the political champion (Tatiana).

**Wedge = AI-voice as a fixed-price, capped, fail-closed line item bundled inside
Unflat activation contracts.** Smallest payable unit: the next Sesc cycle deal.

## Premises (locked by Pedro)
- **P1** Wedge = experiential signature voice, Mariclea/Sesc archetype (annual buy).
  Gov / maquininha / API / SAC = expansion, not wedge.
- **P2** Moat = owning a small (1B) pt-BR model. Serving ≈ R$0.02-0.12/min,
  ~10-40× under ElevenLabs, subsidy-independent (SDumont funds *training* only;
  serving is self-funded — confirmed by Pedro: SDU is classic HPC, no live inference).
- **P3** ElevenLabs' barrier in BR = cost **shape + scale** (variable pass-through),
  not quality. Win = "good enough + structurally cheaper + fixed/fail-closed
  packaging," not "beat Maya on day one."
- **P4** Guardrail: concurrent streams/GPU at <800ms is the unmeasured number that
  decides margin (Phase 0). FINEP/Tatiana/LNCC = de-risk + upside, not foundation.

## New product requirement (emerged here)
Customer-dictated commercial shape: **fixed annual price + quota metering +
fail-closed mode** (AI hard-stops when the weekly/contract quota is exhausted;
no variable bill). This is only economically possible because of P2. It is a
first-class feature, not a billing detail. Feeds back into the constitution.

## Approaches Considered
### Approach A: Bundle now (minimal viable) — CHOSEN as the entry
Sell the voice as an embedded, fixed-price, fail-closed line in Unflat activation
contracts (Sesc model). Runs on the R$50k RTX 5090 box. Reuses warm relationships.
Revenue as early as the next Sesc cycle. Effort S. Risk: lumpy/project-based;
Unflat owns venue reliability for multi-week runs.

### Approach B: Productized "signature voice" — CHOSEN as the evolution
Standardized fixed-annual/capped/fail-closed offering for the experiential
segment; studios (2050/Krya) as channel, venues (Sesc) direct; quota console +
contracts; website demo as funnel. Compounds (each client = template). Funded by
A's cash. Effort M.

### Approach C: Sovereign platform first — REJECTED for now
Lead with Fala Cidadão + FINEP (gov/enterprise, biggest TAM). Political/champion-
dependent, slowest to revenue, contradicts P1/P4. Parking lot expansion only.

## Recommended Approach
**A → B.** A is the de-risked wedge with a named deferred PO sitting in it;
B is the company, funded by A. C stays parked, consistent with P1/P4.

## Success Criteria
- Close the next Sesc cycle with the bundled AI-voice line (the deferred PO).
- Serving cost measured ≤ R$0.12/min at the venue's concurrency, <800ms (Phase 0).
- Fail-closed + quota metering shipped before the first paid run.
- At least 2 of {2050, Krya, VTEX Day} converted from warm access to a scoped pilot.

## Distribution Plan
No app-store / package distribution. Delivery = bundled into existing Unflat
contracts (A) → standardized offer + website freemium demo as funnel (B).
Infra: R$50k RTX 5090 box for MVP/venue runs; cloud/colo when concurrency or
multi-venue SLA demands it (post-Phase-0 number decides).

## Dependencies
- Phase 0 of the technical roadmap (architecture + the streams/GPU number).
- Fail-closed/quota requirement must enter the roadmap (run `/sdd` ATUALIZAR or
  add as a feature-spec — do not silently rewrite the roadmap here).
- Reliability for single-venue multi-week runs (lighter than multi-tenant API,
  but real; the constitution defers "production API/scale" — single-venue
  reliability is nearer-term and should be scoped).

## Open Questions
- Mariclea's next cycle timing/budget envelope for the bundled line.
- Price point of the fixed annual line that is both a clear discount vs ElevenLabs
  and fat-margin at owned serving cost.
- Channel economics with studios (2050/Krya) — referral vs resale vs white-label.
